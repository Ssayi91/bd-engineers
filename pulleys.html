Industrial Grade 'V' Pulleys

Our transmission 'V' pulleys are produced from phosphate GG25 grade cast iron and are dynamically balanced for smooth operation. Available in both taper and pilot bore configurations, they are designed to meet the most demanding industrial specifications.

  • Conforms to ISO 4183 international standards
  • Manufactured from high-grade phosphate GG25 cast iron
  • Dynamically balanced for reduced vibration and extended bearing life
  • Available in Section A, B, and C 'V' belt profiles
  • Custom and special pulley configurations available on request
Material Phosphate GG25 Grade Cast Iron
Configuration Taper Bore & Pilot Bore
Belt Sections A, B, C (Custom sizes available)
Standard ISO 4183 Compliant
Application Conveyor systems, agricultural machinery, industrial drives
