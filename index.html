How do you ensure product quality?

All our machinery and components, from Accura CTC machines to LHP pumpsets, undergo rigorous quality control checks. We source directly from certified global manufacturers and conduct pre-delivery inspections to ensure every product meets international industrial standards.

Do you provide delivery and installation?

Yes, we offer comprehensive delivery services across Kenya and East Africa. Our installation team provides on-site setup, alignment, and operator training to ensure your equipment runs optimally from day one. We handle everything from logistics to commissioning.

What technical support do you offer?

We provide dedicated technical support via phone and WhatsApp. Our engineers can troubleshoot issues remotely and dispatch technicians for on-site repairs when needed. We also offer preventive maintenance contracts to minimize downtime.

What is your warranty policy?

All our products come with manufacturer warranty covering defects in materials and workmanship. Warranty periods vary by product category. We also offer extended warranty options and genuine spare parts supply throughout the equipment lifecycle.

What are your payment terms?

We offer flexible payment terms including bank transfer and M-Pesa. Standard terms are a deposit with balance upon delivery. For established clients and government contracts, we offer structured credit facilities. Contact our sales team for customized solutions.
